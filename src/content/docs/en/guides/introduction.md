Card Workspace is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) plugin for capturing, gathering, organizing, and reframing notes. It turns notes into a readable card stream: on desktop it sits in the **left sidebar** beside the editor, and on phones and tablets it opens in the main workspace. Each Markdown card shows a title and a light excerpt with cues for links, lists, and tasks, so you can understand a set of notes without reducing it to filenames.

The stream can come from a folder, a card box, or the outgoing links and backlinks around a note. Local search works in every source, and tag and property filters narrow folders and card boxes. The [navigation guide](./navigation.md) explains how those sources stay together in one compact pane.

Markdown cards show a [local image preview](./browsing-cards.md#card-images) when the note body embeds a supported image. Image files, PDFs, audio, video, Canvas, and Excalidraw files can appear as cards too; Canvas and Excalidraw show a small diagram.

Click a card to open its note; switch notes in the editor and the matching card is selected. Card boxes collect notes without moving them. When you do want to reorganize, drag cards onto a folder, tag, property value, or card box in the navigation column.

## A four-part workflow

- **Capture** — jot an idea into [quick input](./quick-input-and-templates.md), create a note that inherits the current tags and properties, or start from a template.
- **Gather** — browse folders and card boxes with tags and frontmatter properties, or follow linked notes.
- **Organize** — sort, group, pin, and save a useful view as a [card box](./card-boxes.md); drag cards onto navigation entries to move or tag them.
- **Reframe** — open context beside your work or drag a card into the note you are writing.

## Who it is for

Card Workspace is for people who scan and collect notes while writing: research topics, long-running projects, reading lists, and any body of work that crosses folder boundaries. It gives you direct controls instead of introducing another query language.

## Requirements

- **Desktop, Android, and iOS/iPadOS.** See [Phones and tablets](./mobile.md) for how the mobile layout differs.
- **Obsidian 1.11.4 or later.** Vaults on an older Obsidian stay on Card Workspace 1.3.4. Obsidian 1.13 or later adds settings search and native confirmation dialogs.
- The interface follows Obsidian’s language: Simplified Chinese for languages beginning with `zh`, otherwise English.
