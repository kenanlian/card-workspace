This group contains **1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.4, 1.2.5, and 1.2.6**, clarifying organization and reducing repeated work in indexing and large sources.

## Mixed favorites and task counts

1.2.3 changed favorites to one list that can be reordered across kinds. Folders, notes, tags, and card boxes can follow your working order.

Card task status also became **completed / total**, such as `2/5`, making progress easier to read. See [Navigation](../guides/navigation.md) and [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md).

## Clear browse-filter scope

1.2.3 restricted tag and property browse filters to folder sources. In card boxes and linked-note sources, existing conditions pause with a hint when applicable, then resume on returning to a folder. Card-box membership rules still apply their own tag and property conditions.

See [Property filters](../guides/property-filters.md), [Card boxes](../guides/card-boxes.md), and [Linked notes](../guides/linked-notes.md).

## Group by the complete tag set

1.2.4 corrected tag grouping to use every tag on a note rather than its first tag. `#research` and `#research #draft` form different groups; tag order within the same set does not change its group.

See [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md) for grouping.

## Reuse the index and load cards on demand

1.2.5 corrected index persistence and repeated rebuilds. 1.2.6 further reused indexes for unchanged vaults, reduced redundant writes, and bounded startup and large-source loading work for smoother folder switches.

The index stays local; initial indexing still takes time. See [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md), [Index recovery commands](../reference/commands-and-menus.md), and [Limits and privacy](../reference/limits-and-privacy.md).
