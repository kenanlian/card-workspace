This group contains **1.1.2, 1.1.3, 1.1.4, 1.1.5, and 1.1.6**, with improvements to Chinese search, card previews, and navigation.

## Continuous Chinese search

1.1.2 added deterministic Chinese tokenization using individual characters and adjacent pairs, with mixed Chinese and English queries supported. Search remains local and does not depend on pinyin indexing or an external service.

See [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md) for search scope and everyday use.

## More timely previews and state updates

1.1.3 adjusted updates so settings changes appear promptly in the view. 1.1.4 improved on-demand loading, preview caching, search-index coordination, and scroll preservation. 1.1.5 further corrected excerpt processing and search highlighting.

These changes support folder switching, fast scrolling, and edits to notes. See [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md) for previews and search, and [Limits and privacy](../reference/limits-and-privacy.md) for local data handling.

## Clearer navigation and task status

1.1.6 improved row spacing, hover tooltips, keyboard controls, and resizing, and separated expansion arrows from folder icons. Section header menus let you move sections up or down, with their order and collapsed state remembered.

This release also displayed task status on cards. 1.2.3 later refined it into explicit completed and total counts.

See [Navigation](../guides/navigation.md), [Browsing cards](../guides/browsing-cards.md), and [Settings](../reference/settings.md).
